## 2. Requirements Overview & Scope Mapping

### 2.1 Scope Overview
The platform is structured as a dual-application architecture:
- **Admin Portal** (`admin.example.com`): Admin workflows for uploading textbooks, triggering AI processing pipelines, reviewing/approving generated videos and quizzes, managing users, and configuring thresholds.
- **Learning Portal** (`learn.example.com`): Client interface for students, parents, and teachers to consume content, take quizzes, view analytics, and track mastery.

### 2.2 MVP Scope (Phase 1)
The MVP scope consists strictly of the 8 core features defined in `01_PRD.md` and `README.md`:
1. PDF upload & AI-driven content extraction
2. Automated 5–6 min video generation (pre-rendered)
3. Auto-generated quizzes per topic
4. Admin review workflow before publishing
5. Student portal with video playback, progress tracking, and quiz attempts
6. Basic adaptive quiz (new questions for weak concepts)
7. Read-only parent & teacher dashboards
8. Configurable mastery thresholds and adaptive settings

### 2.3 Phase 1 vs. Phase 2 Feature Scope Distinction
To maintain clear boundary definitions between MVP capabilities and post-MVP enhancements:
- **Basic Adaptive Quizzes** (generating new questions for weak concepts based on performance) and **Read-Only Parent & Teacher Dashboards** are core MVP (Phase 1) features.
- **Adaptive Quiz Engine Enhancements** and **Advanced Parent & Teacher Analytics** are post-MVP capabilities deferred to Phase 2.

### 2.4 Post-MVP Roadmap Overview (Phases 2–4)
- **Phase 2**: Adaptive quiz engine enhancements, advanced teacher & parent analytics
- **Phase 3**: Advanced AI refinements, video streaming CDN, multi-language support
- **Phase 4**: Scaling, CI/CD, monitoring, security hardening

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 PDF Ingestion & Content Extraction
- **REQ-FR-001 (PDF Upload):** The system shall allow administrators to upload textbook PDFs via the Admin portal (`admin.example.com`).
- **REQ-FR-002 (OCR & Content Extraction):** The system shall perform OCR and AI-driven content extraction on ingested textbook PDFs.
- **REQ-FR-003 (Hierarchical Content Extraction):** The system shall extract textbook content into a hierarchical structure consisting of chapters, topics, and subtopics.

### 3.2 Content Segmentation & Video Generation
- **REQ-FR-004 (Content Segmentation):** The system shall segment extracted content into learning units targeting ~5–6 minutes of video duration.
- **REQ-FR-005 (Script & Video Synthesis):** The system shall generate scripts from segmented content and synthesize pre-rendered learning videos targeting 5–6 minutes in duration.

### 3.3 Assessment & Quiz Generation
- **REQ-FR-006 (Topic Quiz Generation):** The system shall automatically generate quizzes aligned to learning objectives for each topic.
- **REQ-FR-007 (Basic Adaptive Quiz):** The system shall provide basic adaptive quizzes that generate new questions for weak concepts based on student performance.

### 3.4 Admin Review & Governance
- **REQ-FR-008 (Admin Review Workflow):** The system shall provide an admin review workflow allowing administrators to review and approve generated videos and quizzes before publishing.
- **REQ-FR-009 (User Management):** The system shall enable administrators to manage platform users within the Admin portal.
- **REQ-FR-010 (Threshold & Adaptive Configuration):** The system shall allow administrators to configure mastery thresholds and adaptive assessment settings.

### 3.5 Student Learning Portal & Dashboards
- **REQ-FR-011 (Student Portal Features):** The system shall provide a Learning portal (`learn.example.com`) enabling students to play pre-rendered videos, track their progress, and record quiz attempts.
- **REQ-FR-012 (Read-Only Dashboards):** The system shall provide read-only dashboards on the Learning portal for parents and teachers to monitor student progress and track mastery.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- **REQ-NFR-001 (Target Video Duration):** Video units generated by the AI pipeline must target a duration of 5–6 minutes.
- **REQ-NFR-002 (Pre-Rendered Video Delivery):** Generated learning videos must be pre-rendered prior to publishing.

## 6. System Constraints & Boundary Conditions

- **CON-001 (Input Scope Constraint):** Content ingestion for the MVP is scoped to textbook PDF files.
- **CON-002 (Publishing Dependency):** Video content must be pre-rendered and reviewed/approved by an administrator prior to publishing.
- **CON-003 (Dual-Application Boundary):** The system architecture is constrained to two distinct web entry points: `admin.example.com` (Admin portal) and `learn.example.com` (Learning portal).

## 7. Assumptions

No explicit assumptions are defined in the source `README.md` or locked `01_PRD.md`.

## 8. Post-MVP & Deferred Requirements

The following requirements are deferred to post-MVP roadmap phases:
- **Phase 2 Deferred**: Adaptive quiz engine enhancements, advanced teacher & parent analytics.
- **Phase 3 Deferred**: Advanced AI pipeline refinements, dedicated video streaming CDN, multi-language support.
- **Phase 4 Deferred**: Platform scaling, automated CI/CD pipelines, operational monitoring, security hardening.
